About me

01

I grew up drawing on my dad's shop receipts. Now I design whole brands, and I still love a good receipt.

I'm studying for a BFA in Graphic Design at Karnaphuli Institute of Fine Arts. I'm happiest when a brief is small and specific: a tea stall that needs a logo, a club that needs posters by Friday, a home bakery that needs a box people keep.

I like bold colour, strong grids and Bangla lettering, and I care just as much about the boring parts: file naming, print specs and handing over clean source files.

Right now I'm looking for a 3–6 month design internship at a studio or in-house brand team, in Chattogram, Dhaka or remote.

Cha Kotha — tea stall identity

Twelve posters and a social kit for a campus indie music festival. One flexible grid, three colours, a custom display type. Printed as risograph posters and animated for Instagram.

Mishti Ghor — sweet box packaging

A fold-flat box family for a home bakery, in three sizes from one die-line. Patterns are drawn from nakshi kantha stitching. Modelled and rendered in Blender before the first print run.
