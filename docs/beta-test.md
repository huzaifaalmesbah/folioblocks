# FolioBlocks student beta

Thank you for trying FolioBlocks. It takes about 20 minutes. You need a laptop or phone and a browser; nothing to install.

**Open:** https://huzaifaalmesbah.github.io/folioblocks/

## What to do

Tick each one as you go. If something confuses you or breaks, that is exactly what we want to hear: note it and carry on.

1. [ ] On the home page, pick a template and press **Use this template**.
2. [ ] In **1. Info**, fill in **The basics** (name, tagline, bio, photo, email). Watch the preview change.
3. [ ] Try **✨ Fill with AI**: copy the prompt, paste it with your CV or a few notes into any AI chat, and paste the answer back. Did it fill the form correctly?
4. [ ] Add at least two projects with a picture each, and your education.
5. [ ] In **2. Style**, change the colours and fonts. If you write in Bengali, set **Site language** to বাংলা.
6. [ ] In **3. Layout**, drag a section to a new place, remove one, and add one.
7. [ ] Press **Open in WordPress Playground** and click around the real WordPress site (Home, About, Blog, a post, Contact).
8. [ ] Press **Download**, read the checklist, fix what it lists, and download the zip.
9. [ ] If you have a WordPress site (the free WordPress.com Student plan works), upload the zip in **Appearance → Themes → Add Theme → Upload Theme** and activate it.
10. [ ] On a phone, open the builder and switch between **Edit** and **Preview**.

## Tell us

Open a feedback form (you need a free GitHub account): https://github.com/huzaifaalmesbah/folioblocks/issues/new?template=beta-feedback.yml

Or answer these and send them to your organiser:

- Which template did you choose, and why?
- What was the hardest or most confusing step?
- Did anything look wrong in the preview, in Playground, or on your real site? (A screenshot helps a lot.)
- Did "Fill with AI" get anything wrong? Which AI chat did you use?
- What is missing for your portfolio: a field, a section, a template?
- Phone or laptop? Which browser?
- Would you publish this site as your portfolio? If not, what stops you?

## For organisers

- A group of 5–10 students is enough for a first round.
- Ask students not to share private details (phone number, home address) in feedback or screenshots.
- Everything stays in the student's browser; "Save draft (.json)" lets them move their work to another computer.
- Known limits: the in-app preview is a close copy of WordPress, not WordPress itself (Playground is the real check); the app's own interface is in English.
